How to install

  1. Tap Download FormIQ above. If your phone asks, tap Download again.
  2. When it finishes, tap Open on the message at the bottom of the screen. Or open the Files app, go to Downloads and tap FormIQ.apk.
  3. If your phone says it can't install apps from this source, tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, then tap the back arrow.
  4. Tap Install. If Google Play Protect asks to scan the app, tap Scan app and then Install.
  5. Tap Open. FormIQ is now on your home screen.

Why does my phone show a warning?

Android shows a warning for any app that doesn't come from the Play Store. FormIQ is built automatically on GitHub from code anyone can read, and every version is signed with the same key, so your phone will only accept genuine updates.

Updating

FormIQ tells you when a new version is ready. Tap Download update and install it the same way. Your workouts, foods and settings are kept.

Your privacy

The app has no ads, no tracking and no account. Your camera, workouts, steps and meals stay on your phone. It asks for each permission (camera, Bluetooth, physical activity, Health Connect) only when you use that feature. Read the full Privacy Policy.

Using an iPhone or a computer?

Open FormIQ in your browser instead. Chrome works best for Bluetooth machines.
