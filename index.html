          Looking for you

          Works with FitShow and other smart treadmills, bikes and trainers that use the standard Bluetooth fitness-machine protocol. Breathing rate needs a chest strap that sends beat-to-beat data (for example Polar H10, Garmin HRM, Wahoo TICKR).

              Before you start

              • FormIQ gives automated form feedback and food scores. They are not medical or dietary advice and can be wrong. Always check allergens on the package. Check with a doctor before starting a new program, and stop if anything hurts.
              • Your camera is analyzed on this device only. Video is never recorded or uploaded. Barcodes and food searches are looked up on Open Food Facts. Workout, food and step history stay in this browser. Strava and photo analysis are off unless you connect them.
              • Clear the space around you and set the camera somewhere stable. On a treadmill, keep the safety clip on: FormIQ never changes its speed.

              Photo meal analysis

              This optional feature sends a meal photo directly from your device to an AI provider you choose, using your own API key. FormIQ has no server and never sees your photo, key or results.

              • Photos are shrunk and stripped of location and camera data before sending.
              • Nothing is sent until you review the photo and tap Send, every time.
              • Only food is analyzed; the photo is discarded after the reply. Keep people out of the shot.
              • Your key stays on this device: for this session only, unless you choose to remember it.
