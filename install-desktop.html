Get FormIQ for your computer

Free, no ads, no account. Works offline. Your camera video never leaves your computer. Phone? Get the Android app.

Linux

Download for Linux (.zip)
  1. Download the zip above and unzip it (right-click, Extract here).
  2. Open the new folder and double-click formiq. Or in a terminal: ./formiq
  3. Click Start camera and allow it. Done.

Other Linux downloads: AppImage (single file: right-click, Properties, allow running as a program; Arch/CachyOS needs sudo pacman -S fuse2), .deb for Ubuntu/Debian/Mint, .tar.gz.

If it says the sandbox can't start, your system has unprivileged user namespaces switched off. Turn them on (sudo sysctl kernel.unprivileged_userns_clone=1) or run ./formiq --no-sandbox once.

Windows

Download for Windows (installer)

Prefer no install? Download the zip, unzip it and run FormIQ.exe. Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the app isn't code-signed: click More info, then Run anyway.

Mac

Download for Mac (Apple chip)

Older Intel Mac? Intel version. Open the .dmg and drag FormIQ to Applications. The first time, right-click the app and choose Open, because it isn't code-signed.

Tips

Prop a laptop or webcam about 2 to 3 metres away. If you have more than one camera, press Switch camera to cycle through them. Treadmills and bikes with Bluetooth connect from the Ride tab: FormIQ lists nearby devices and only connects to the one you choose.

Updating

Download the newest file from this page and replace the old one. Your workouts, foods and settings stay on your computer.

Your privacy

No ads, no tracking, no account, no crash reports. The app only uses your camera (never the microphone), and only after you press Start. Read the full Privacy Policy. Every build is made automatically on GitHub from code anyone can read; checksums are published with each release.
