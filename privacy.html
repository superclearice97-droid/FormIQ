Privacy Policy

Effective October 9, 2026 · Updated October 9, 2026 to cover the Food features, self-hosting, optional photo analysis, and Activity (steps, goals and connections)

Short version: FormIQ analyzes your camera on your own device. Video and body-tracking data are never uploaded, stored on a server, sold or shared. When you look up a food, only the barcode number or search words are sent to Open Food Facts. Photo meal analysis is off unless you turn it on with your own AI key. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking cookies, and the app loads all of its code, fonts and models from its own site.

Who runs FormIQ

FormIQ is an independent project published by its developer (GitHub user superclearice97-droid) at superclearice97-droid.github.io/FormIQ. Questions or requests: open an issue at https://github.com/superclearice97-droid/FormIQ/issues.

Camera and video

When you tap Start camera, your browser asks for permission. If you allow it, video frames go to a pose-tracking model that runs inside your browser on your device. Frames are analyzed and discarded right away. They are not recorded, saved or sent anywhere. The same applies to a video file you choose with Analyze a video: it is read on your device and never uploaded.

The tracker estimates the positions of body joints (shoulders, elbows, hips, knees and so on) to measure joint angles. It does not identify who you are, does not use facial recognition, and keeps no body or face measurements after each frame is processed.

Food scanning and search

The barcode scanner reads the barcode on your device; camera frames are not saved or sent anywhere. To show product details, the app sends only the barcode number (or the words you type into food search) to Open Food Facts, an open food database run by a non-profit. Product photos are loaded from Open Food Facts' image servers. These requests include your IP address and browser information, as any web request does, and are handled under the Open Food Facts privacy policy. FormIQ does not send them your workouts, meals, health information or anything that identifies you.

Workout history and settings

Saved workouts (exercise, reps, accuracy score and the main form cue for each set), your food library (products you looked up and their scores), meals you build and your settings are stored in your browser's local storage on your device. They are never sent to us. You can delete individual lists with their Clear buttons, erase everything FormIQ stored on this device with Delete all my data at the bottom of the app, or clear this site's data in your browser settings.

Steps, goals and connections

  • Step counting uses your phone's motion sensor only while FormIQ is open and you've tapped Count steps. Motion readings are turned into a step count on your device and immediately discarded.
  • Apple Health and CSV imports (Google Takeout, Samsung Health and others) are read entirely on your device. The file is never uploaded. FormIQ keeps only daily step totals and basic workout details (type, date, duration, distance, energy) from the last year, and ignores everything else in the file, such as heart rate or medical records.
  • Treadmills and bikes (FitShow and other Bluetooth fitness machines) connect directly to your device over Bluetooth. No internet is involved.
  • Strava is optional. You connect it with your own Strava API application, whose Client ID, Client Secret and access tokens are stored only on this device. When you sync, FormIQ downloads your activities from the last 60 days and, if you leave the option on, sends your FormIQ strength workouts (exercise names, sets, reps, time and duration) to your Strava account. Strava handles that data under the Strava Privacy Policy. Disconnect revokes FormIQ's access at Strava and deletes the keys and synced activities from this device.
  • Goals and daily totals are stored only on this device.
  • Export my data downloads everything FormIQ stores on this device as a file, so you can keep or move it. API keys and tokens are left out of the file.

Optional photo meal analysis

This feature is off by default and only works if you turn it on and enter your own API key for an AI provider (Anthropic's Claude or Google's Gemini). When it is on:

  • Nothing is sent until you choose a photo, review it, and tap Send. Each photo needs its own tap.
  • Before sending, the photo is shrunk to at most 768 pixels and re-encoded, which removes location (GPS), camera and other hidden metadata.
  • The photo goes directly from your device to the provider you chose. FormIQ has no server, and never receives your photo, your key or the results.
  • The provider processes the photo under its own terms and privacy policy, which you agree to with them when you create your key: Anthropic Privacy Policy, Gemini API Terms. Anthropic does not train on API inputs by default. Google may use free-tier Gemini inputs to improve its products, including human review; a paid-tier key avoids this.
  • The photo is discarded after the reply. Only the resulting food estimates are saved, in your food library on this device.
  • Your key is kept only on this device: for the current browser session, or until you delete it if you choose “Remember my key.” “Turn off and forget key” erases it and switches the feature off.
  • Please keep people out of meal photos. The app asks the AI to look only at food.

The app's security settings block connections to any other site except the ones below, and only when you use the matching feature. Voice cues

Spoken rep counts use your browser's built-in speech feature. Depending on your browser and device, speech may be generated by your operating system or a voice service your browser uses. Turn it off with the Voice button.

What we do not do

We do not collect, sell or share personal information, consumer health data or biometric identifiers. Your food choices, meals and photos are never sent to us; meal photos go only to the AI provider you choose, and only if you turn that feature on. We do not use cookies for tracking or advertising, and we do not build profiles.

Children

FormIQ is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone. Because the app keeps no data off your device, there is nothing for us to delete; a parent can clear the app's data in the browser.

Your choices and rights

You can deny or revoke camera permission at any time in your browser. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR or US state privacy laws), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data. Since FormIQ holds no personal data about you, these requests are fulfilled by clearing the app's data on your device. You can still contact us through the link above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. If the app ever begins collecting data, this policy will be updated and you will be asked for consent first.
